# Store Factory working draft

This is a functional draft without owner-supplied visual direction. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. It is not a final brand design.

The agency operator's main task is selecting a store and editing its catalogue. The workspace uses a store register and a focused editor, without decorative analytics or invented revenue.

- Paper and forest-green colours refer to a shop's catalogue and inventory ledger. Green marks primary actions. The dark theme is an operator preference with the same hierarchy.
- System sans-serif makes forms readable without font downloads; Georgia is reserved for the storefront's editorial template and the workspace title.
- Repeated numbered catalogue entries connect the store register and product list. All sample catalogues are visibly identified as examples.
- Equal store rows support comparison. Product cards support browsing real catalogue fields, not feature marketing.
- Contact fields start empty. Sample stores use names from the brief, with clearly labelled sample products; no unverified phone numbers are used.
- Brand colours are confined to borders and non-text accents so arbitrary client colour choices do not compromise text contrast.
- Content-driven wrapping replaces the desktop split view on narrow screens. Controls have at least 44px targets. No ambient animation or generated imagery.
