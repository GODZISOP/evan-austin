# Morrow Studio

## Direction

- Identity: an independent concept creative studio making brands and digital experiences.
- Audience: people looking for a web or brand partner with a strong visual point of view.
- Personality: direct, expressive, deliberate.
- Visual reference: XOneHub's midnight-purple canvas, bright type, violet accents, and image-led drama. The composition and copy here are original, not a replica.
- Palette: midnight aubergine `#0b002d`, off-white `#f7f4ff`, electric violet `#8e54ff`, and light lilac `#caaaff`. Violet is the action and emphasis accent.
- Typography: Arial/Helvetica keeps the large headlines crisp and modern; Georgia italic interrupts the geometry at important phrases, giving Morrow its own voice.
- Motif: angular chrome/acrylic images and interrupted italic phrases repeat from hero to project studies.
- Layout: image and type share the hero; a selectable service index, two different study formats, a full-color statement, and a functional contact draft give each section a different job.
- Motion: Framer Motion's `motion/react` drives staged headline entry, image reveals, scroll-linked parallax, a scroll-linked type band, service-panel handoffs, and section reveals. Reduced-motion preferences remove the scroll effects and transforms.
- Theme: the fixed midnight-purple theme is intentional because the user explicitly requested the reference's theme; no light mode is implied by this visual identity.

## Decision notes

- Deep purple keeps the requested reference mood while making the bright imagery and type legible.
- Lilac italic marks the emotional turn in key headlines, so the serif contrast has a purpose rather than being decorative everywhere.
- Generated chrome/acrylic images illustrate the studio's visual thinking. They are explicitly concept studies, not client work or product screenshots.
- Services are an interactive index because visitors can compare disciplines without scrolling through four repeated cards.
- The wide work image and split second study create a different reading rhythm while keeping both studies honest.
- The violet statement panel is the emotional peak; the rest of the page stays dark to make that moment distinct.
- The contact form creates and copies an inquiry draft because no real delivery endpoint or studio email was supplied.
